# Interests

The curated topic list the Media Desk weights its picks toward. The operator
evolves this over time — edit it directly, or (if the agent is interactive) ask
it in-channel to add/drop/reweight a topic. Order roughly reflects priority.

## Core

- **AI startups building specific products** — companies shipping a concrete
  thing (agents, dev tools, vertical SaaS, infra), who it's for, why now.
- **Frontier-lab tooling & releases** — new models, APIs, and developer tools
  and what they unlock for builders.
- **Venture capital** — funding rounds, fund dynamics, thesis-level takes.
- **Fintech** — payments, infra, embedded finance, the AI × fintech overlap.

## Also relevant

- **Policy & government** — regulation, antitrust, export controls, and gov
  events that actually move the industry. Substance over theater.
- **How builders build** — founders and engineers explaining architecture,
  product decisions, and lessons from shipping.

## Not interested

- Consumer tech reviews, price speculation, culture-war content, and pure
  hype/reaction with no substance.
